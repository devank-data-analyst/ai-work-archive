Process & Discipline

How I Work with AI

A repeatable methodology prioritizing problem clarity, tool selection, iterative refinement, and human verification.

Step 01

Understand the Task

Clarify human objectives, input constraints, edge cases, and determine whether AI is truly the right tool.

Step 02

Select Models & Tools

Match strengths: reasoning vs speed, multimodal vs text, open-weights vs hosted APIs based on the task.

Step 03

Design Workflows

Structure input prompts, few-shot examples, JSON schemas, and multi-turn decomposition strategies.

Step 04

Validate & Combine

Critically verify generated results, catch subtle hallucinations, and combine outputs with conventional code.

Step 05

Deliver & Document

Package the solution into maintainable software, clear case studies, and transparent limitations.

About the Practitioner

Learning in Public,
Grounded in Practice.

I’m Devank Gupta, an Applied AI Generalist focused on putting generative models and LLMs to real work. My approach centers on judgment: understanding where foundation models excel, identifying where they struggle, and designing workflows that produce dependable results.

Rather than treating AI as a novelty chat companion or claiming to train frontier models from scratch, I bridge the space between raw AI capabilities and practical outcomes across research, data analysis, automation, and creative assets.

1. Judgment Over Hype
Select tools based on verifiable task fit, not excitement or promotional benchmarks.
2. Critical Verification
Every AI output must be audited, validated, and paired with deterministic safeguards.
3. Usable Outcomes
Work is complete when it solves a real problem and can be inspected by stakeholders.
4. Honest Attribution
Clearly distinguish human decisions, AI-assisted tools, and automated outputs.
